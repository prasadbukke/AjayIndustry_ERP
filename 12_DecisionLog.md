# Decision 044 - GRN Uses Purchase Order as Source Transaction

GRN - Goods Receipt Note is the material receipt transaction against Supplier Purchase Order.

Relationship:

One Purchase Order
→ Multiple GRNs

GRN supports:

- Partial receipt
- Full receipt
- GRN History against Purchase Order
- Material receipt status
- Same Supplier + Same Challan duplicate prevention
- Latest GRN edit rules
- Purchase Order locking rules after GRN creation

Inventory stock effect is handled through the appropriate inventory transaction integration.

---

# Decision 045 - Customer Code Is System Generated

Customer Code is generated by the system.

Format:

`AI/CUS/00001`

Customer Code is permanent and should not be manually entered by the user.

Deleted Customer Codes are not reused.

---

# Decision 046 - Customer Master Uses Simple Phase 1 Structure

Current Customer Master maintains the primary business information required for Sales transactions.

Phase 1 uses:

- One primary contact
- One primary address

Multiple contacts and multiple addresses are intentionally deferred until required.

---

# Decision 047 - Customer GSTIN Is Optional

Customer GSTIN is optional.

A Customer may exist even when GSTIN is not available.

However, when GSTIN is provided, duplicate GSTIN is blocked.

---

# Decision 048 - Customer Duplicate Contact Information Is Blocked

Customer Master prevents duplicate values where applicable for:

- GSTIN
- Email
- Mobile Number

This reduces accidental duplicate Customer creation.

---

# Decision 049 - Customer Credit Limit Is Deferred

Customer Master does not currently enforce Credit Limit.

Credit Limit and credit-control rules are deferred to a future Accounts / Receivables phase.

Financial balances must come from transaction data rather than static Customer Master values.

---

# Decision 050 - Customer Purchase Order Is the Main Incoming Sales Transaction

Customer Purchase Order is the main incoming business transaction for Customer orders.

The Sales / Production flow begins from:

Customer
→ Customer Purchase Order
→ Customer Purchase Order Items

The Customer Purchase Order becomes the source for downstream Production and Sales transactions.

---

# Decision 051 - Existing Item Master Is Reused for Customer Products

A separate Product Master is not introduced.

Customer Purchase Order Items reference the existing Item Master.

Reason:

The existing Item architecture already provides:

- Item identity
- Part Number
- Shape
- Specifications
- Drawing information
- UOM
- Technical traceability

Creating a second Product Master would duplicate the same engineering data.

---

# Decision 052 - Customer Purchase Order Code Is Financial-Year Based

Customer Purchase Order has a system generated business Code.

The Code follows the Financial Year sequence pattern used by ERP transaction documents.

Financial Year:

April to March

Deleted document Codes are not reused.

The Customer's own Purchase Order Number remains a separate business reference.

---

# Decision 053 - Same Customer and Customer PO Number Cannot Be Duplicated

The combination:

Customer
+ Customer Purchase Order Number

must be unique among applicable active business records.

This prevents the same incoming Customer PO from being registered multiple times accidentally.

---

# Decision 054 - Same Item May Appear on Multiple Customer PO Lines

Customer Purchase Order does not enforce Item uniqueness across lines.

The same Item may appear on multiple lines when required by the Customer PO.

Each line remains an independent Customer Purchase Order Item transaction line.

---

# Decision 055 - Customer Purchase Order Uses Header + Line Architecture

Customer Purchase Order follows the standard ERP transaction structure:

CustomerPurchaseOrder
→ Header

CustomerPurchaseOrderItem
→ Lines

One Customer Purchase Order may contain multiple Items.

---

# Decision 056 - Customer Purchase Order Draft Is Editable

Customer Purchase Order editing is controlled by document workflow.

Draft:

- Editable
- May be processed according to Draft rules

Confirmed:

- Normal editing is blocked

This protects downstream Production references from source transaction changes.

---

# Decision 057 - Confirmed Customer Purchase Orders May Still Be Soft Deleted

Customer Purchase Order uses Soft Delete.

Confirmed Customer Purchase Orders may be soft deleted when permitted by business validation.

Deleted Customer Purchase Orders are displayed separately in the Deleted Orders UI.

Physical deletion is avoided.

---

# Decision 058 - Customer PO Restore Preserves Previous Status

Restoring a deleted Customer Purchase Order does not reset its workflow status automatically.

Restore preserves the status that existed before deletion.

Examples:

Draft
→ Delete
→ Restore
→ Draft

Confirmed
→ Delete
→ Restore
→ Confirmed

---

# Decision 059 - Production Data Is Not Stored Directly on Customer PO

Machine and Production execution fields are not stored directly on Customer Purchase Order Header or Lines.

Customer Purchase Order represents the Customer's commercial requirement.

Production execution belongs to the Production module.

Relationship:

Customer Purchase Order Item
→ Production Job

This separation prevents Production lifecycle logic from contaminating the Sales order transaction.

---

# Decision 060 - Production Job Is Linked to Customer PO Item

Production Job is created against a Customer Purchase Order Item.

This provides traceability from Production back to:

Production Job
→ Customer Purchase Order Item
→ Customer Purchase Order
→ Customer

Production therefore remains directly connected to the original Customer requirement.

---

# Decision 061 - Machine Is a Separate Master

Machine is reusable Master data.

Machine Code is generated using:

`AI/MCH/00001`

Machine records use the standard ERP lifecycle including Soft Delete and Restore.

---

# Decision 062 - Machine Serial Number Is Optional but Unique When Provided

Machine Serial Number is optional.

When a Serial Number is entered, it must be unique according to Machine Master validation.

This allows machines without available manufacturer serial numbers while preventing duplicate registered serial numbers.

---

# Decision 063 - Machine Operational Status Is Manually Maintained

Machine operational status represents the current operational condition of the Machine.

It is maintained separately from Production execution.

Machine operational status is not automatically treated as Production Step Status.

---

# Decision 064 - Machine Status and Production Step Status Are Separate Concepts

Machine Status answers:

What is the current operational condition of this Machine?

Production Step Status answers:

What is the current execution state of this Production operation?

These concepts must remain separate even when a Production Step references a Machine.

---

# Decision 065 - Deleted Machines Have Dedicated Restore Flow

Machine Master uses Soft Delete.

Deleted Machines:

- remain in database
- appear in the Deleted Machines UI
- may be restored

Restore preserves the previous operational status unless specific business validation requires otherwise.

---

# Decision 066 - Production Completion Is the Primary Invoice Eligibility Gate

A Production Job becomes eligible for Invoice only after Production is completed.

Invoice eligibility is therefore based primarily on:

`ProductionJobStatus.Completed`

PDI and Delivery Challan are not the primary eligibility gate for Invoice creation.

---

# Decision 067 - Invoice Source Is Customer Purchase Order and Completed Production

Invoice creation does not start by selecting Delivery Challans.

Current source flow is:

Customer Purchase Order
→ Completed Production Jobs
→ Invoice

User selects a Customer Purchase Order.

The system then loads eligible Production Jobs belonging to that Customer Purchase Order whose Production is completed.

---

# Decision 068 - Delivery Challan Is Not Mandatory for Invoice Creation

An Invoice may be created even when a Delivery Challan has not yet been created for the completed Production Job.

Delivery Challan is therefore not a mandatory Invoice source transaction.

This supports business cases where Billing must proceed independently of Delivery Challan completion.

---

# Decision 069 - Finalized PDI Is Not Mandatory for Invoice Creation

An Invoice may be created even when Finalized PDI does not exist for the completed Production Job.

PDI remains an important process control but is not a hard Invoice creation dependency.

---

# Decision 070 - Missing PDI or Delivery Challan Produces a Warning

When an Invoice contains a completed Production Job for which:

- Finalized PDI is missing

OR

- Delivery Challan is missing

the system displays a warning.

The warning informs the user that the normal PDI / Delivery Challan process is incomplete.

It does not automatically block the Invoice.

---

# Decision 071 - Invoice Warning Requires Explicit User Confirmation

When PDI or Delivery Challan is pending, the user must explicitly confirm that the Invoice should continue.

The UI provides a confirmation checkbox.

Invoice processing / finalization proceeds only after the warning has been acknowledged.

The Application Service revalidates this rule.

Browser-side confirmation alone is not considered authoritative.

---

# Decision 072 - Invoice Does Not Trust Browser Source Data

Invoice source validation is performed in the Application Service.

The server validates:

- Production Job
- Production completion status
- Customer Purchase Order ownership
- available Invoice quantity
- existing Invoice allocation
- warning requirements

Posted browser values are not trusted as authoritative transaction source data.

---

# Decision 073 - Invoice Quantity Cannot Exceed Available Production Quantity

Invoice quantity is validated against the Production quantity available for billing.

Previously invoiced quantity for the same Production Job is considered.

Conceptually:

Available Quantity
=
Production Quantity
-
Already Invoiced Quantity

Invoice quantity cannot exceed the remaining available quantity.

The Application Service performs the authoritative validation.

---

# Decision 074 - Invoice Keeps Delivery Challan References as Optional Historical Data

Existing InvoiceItem Delivery Challan fields are retained for historical compatibility.

They are no longer mandatory for new Invoice lines.

Examples include:

- DeliveryChallanId
- DeliveryChallanCode
- DeliveryChallanItemId
- DeliveryChallanQuantity

These fields are nullable / optional for the new Production-based Invoice flow.

The existing Invoice entity architecture is retained rather than redesigned unnecessarily.

---

# Decision 075 - InvoiceItem Stores Production Job Traceability

InvoiceItem stores the Production Job reference used for billing.

This provides traceability:

Invoice
→ InvoiceItem
→ Production Job
→ Customer Purchase Order Item
→ Customer Purchase Order

Production Job is the authoritative operational source for the Invoice line.

---

# Decision 076 - Invoice Stores Customer PO Traceability on Line Snapshot

InvoiceItem preserves Customer Purchase Order references required for historical traceability.

Snapshot information includes applicable values such as:

- Customer Purchase Order Item
- Customer Purchase Order Code
- Customer Purchase Order Number

Historical Invoice documents therefore remain understandable even when related Master or transaction data changes later.

---

# Decision 077 - Invoice Number Is Financial-Year Based

Invoice Number format:

`AI/INV/{YY-YY}/{00001}`

Example:

`AI/INV/26-27/00001`

Financial Year:

April to March

Invoice Code is generated by the Application Service.

Deleted Invoice Codes are not reused.

---

# Decision 078 - Invoice Financial Calculation Is Server Authoritative

Invoice financial calculations are performed and validated by the Application Service.

Calculations include applicable:

- Quantity
- Rate
- Gross Amount
- Discount
- Taxable Amount
- GST
- CGST
- SGST
- IGST
- Other Charges
- Round Off
- Grand Total

Calculated values posted by the browser are not trusted as final authoritative values.

---

# Decision 079 - Invoice Uses GST Based on Inter-State or Intra-State Transaction

Invoice supports:

Intra-State:

CGST + SGST

Inter-State:

IGST

Invoice presentation displays the applicable GST percentage.

Examples:

`CGST (9%)`
`SGST (9%)`

or

`IGST (18%)`

when the line GST rate is 18%.

Mixed GST rates are represented appropriately when multiple GST rates exist.

---

# Decision 080 - Invoice PDF Uses Saved Historical Snapshots

Invoice PDF uses the saved Invoice and InvoiceItem snapshot information.

The PDF must not depend on current Master values where doing so could change historical Invoice presentation.

Company, Customer and transaction references are preserved appropriately.

---

# Decision 081 - Customer PO Is Displayed in Invoice PDF at Header and Line Level

Customer Purchase Order Number is displayed in the Invoice PDF BILL TO section.

Customer Purchase Order is also displayed as an Item table column.

Reason:

The Customer may require the PO reference at both document level and individual line level.

---

# Decision 082 - HSN Is Displayed as a Separate Invoice PDF Column

Invoice PDF contains a dedicated:

`HSN No.`

column.

HSN is not hidden only inside Item description text.

This improves tax-document readability.

---

# Decision 083 - Production Job Is an Internal Traceability Source

Production Job is an important internal Invoice source and traceability reference.

However, customer-facing Invoice presentation is designed around commercial references such as:

- Customer PO
- Item
- HSN
- Quantity
- Rate
- GST
- Amount

Internal Production information should only be exposed on customer documents when there is a clear business requirement.

---

# Decision 084 - Invoice Draft Can Be Edited and Finalized

Invoice uses a Draft-to-Finalized workflow.

Draft Invoice:

- may be edited
- may be soft deleted
- may be finalized

Finalized Invoice:

- cannot be edited through normal Invoice workflow
- cannot be deleted through normal Draft delete workflow

Finalization represents the accounting/business lock point for the Invoice.

---

# Decision 085 - Invoice Finalization Revalidates Source Rules

Invoice source validation is not performed only during Create.

Finalization revalidates the applicable Production and warning rules.

This prevents a stale Draft Invoice from bypassing current business validation.

---

# Decision 086 - Invoice Uses Shared Create and Edit Form

Invoice Create and Edit share:

`_Form.cshtml`

Invoice form JavaScript remains in a separate file:

`invoice-form.js`

Business/UI behavior should not be duplicated as separate Create and Edit implementations.

---

# Decision 087 - Invoice JavaScript Is UI Assistance Only

`invoice-form.js` handles user-interface behavior such as:

- loading Customer PO Production data
- refreshing available quantities
- calculations for display
- warning presentation
- row re-indexing
- client-side validation assistance

JavaScript does not replace Application Service business validation.

The server remains authoritative.

---

# Decision 088 - Invoice Details Shows Production and Customer PO Traceability

Invoice Details displays the relevant source traceability including:

- Customer Purchase Order
- Production Job

Delivery Challan is no longer treated as the primary Invoice source column.

Historical Delivery Challan references may remain stored internally.

---

Invoice implementation is considered complete after successful testing of:

- Customer PO selection
- Completed Production loading
- Invoice quantity validation
- PDI / Delivery Challan warning
- warning confirmation
- Create
- Edit
- Details
- Finalize
